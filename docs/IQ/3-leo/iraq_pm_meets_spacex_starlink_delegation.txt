Prime Minister Mohammed S. Al-Sudani Meets Delegation from SpaceX (Starlink)
….
Prime Minister Mohammed S. Al-Sudani met today, Thursday, a delegation from SpaceX (Starlink), the U.S. company specializing in network, information, and communications technologies. The meeting was attended by the U.S. Chargé d’Affaires to Iraq, Mr. Joshua Harris.
The discussions addressed the final procedures related to granting satellite internet licenses, including the license designated for SpaceX, as well as avenues for strengthening cooperation in the telecommunications sector, the services provided by the company, and its prospective coverage areas.
The Prime Minister affirmed that Iraq has made significant progress in creating an environment capable of attracting leading global companies and meeting all operational requirements, including access to satellite internet systems essential for sustaining their activities. He underscored the government’s determination to keep pace with global advancements in information and communications technology and the broader digital transformation, through enhanced cooperation with SpaceX and its entry into the Iraqi market, which today presents substantial and promising investment opportunities.
Prime Minister Al-Sudani emphasized that the telecommunications sector in Iraq is governed by clear national priorities, foremost among them preserving Iraq’s technological and information sovereignty, investing in the expertise of young Iraqi professionals, building their capacities, and ultimately entrusting them with operational responsibilities.
For its part, the SpaceX Starlink delegation expressed the company’s strong interest in operating in Iraq, providing services, and developing partnerships in the information technology sector. The delegation affirmed that the company is fully prepared to commence operations and deliver satellite internet services within a short period, pending the issuance of its official operating license in Iraq.

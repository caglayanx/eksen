License Fees, Royalties & Regulatory Charges
Telecom and media operators in Iraq are subject to licensing fees, regulatory charges, spectrum usage fees, numbering fees, and in some cases royalties linked to revenue or usage. These charges support sector supervision, infrastructure development, and management of national communication resources. Fee schedules may vary depending on license category, spectrum allocation, service scope, and network footprint.

Spectrum usage fees

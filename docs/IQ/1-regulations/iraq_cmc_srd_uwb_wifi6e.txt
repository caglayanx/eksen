Iraq CMC Released SRDs and UWB Regulation
On September 29, 2025, The Communications and Media Commission (CMC) of Iraq released a new Regulation on Short-Range Radio Devices (SRDs) and Using Ultra Wideband (UWB) Devices Technology.

 

Iraq has now opened Wi-Fi 6E, with the frequency band of 5945-6425 MHz.

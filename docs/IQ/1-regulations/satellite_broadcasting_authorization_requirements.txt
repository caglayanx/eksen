
Satellite & Broadcasting Authorization Requirements
Satellite-based broadcasting, uplink/downlink services, and satellite news operations require CMC authorization due to spectrum usage, content transmission, and national security considerations. Satellite licensing applies to both domestic broadcasters and foreign satellite channels seeking carriage or transmission rights within Iraq.

Applicant Requirements
Corporate and technical documentation
Satellite footprint and uplink/downlink details
Content and programming descriptions
Compliance with cultural and ethical standards
CMC Compliance
Comply with transponder and coverage requirements
Respect content regulation and monitoring
Maintain broadcast logs and archives
Renew authorizations and update operational data

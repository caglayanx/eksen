The company Starlink Azerbaijan, which had begun operations in the country, has now been officially registered.

According to information obtained by APA-Economics, the company was registered on June 4.

Based on the company’s application, the Information and Communication Technologies Agency confirmed the registration.

Starlink will provide high-speed internet services via satellite in Azerbaijan.

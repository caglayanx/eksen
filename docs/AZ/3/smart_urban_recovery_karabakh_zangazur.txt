Karabakh and East Zangazur are rebuilding through smart villages, sustainable housing and heritage-led urban recovery.
Karabakh and East Zangazur are undergoing large-scale post-conflict reconstruction, combining smart housing, modern infrastructure and heritage preservation. Around 60,000 residents have returned to settlements across the region, supported by new homes, schools, healthcare and employment opportunities. Developments such as Aghali village integrate renewable energy, digital solutions and sustainable planning. In Shusha, restoration efforts draw on detailed historical records to rebuild architecture in harmony with traditional styles. Planners say the goal is to create resilient ecosystems that blend modern design with cultural continuity, as communities re-establish daily life across the region.

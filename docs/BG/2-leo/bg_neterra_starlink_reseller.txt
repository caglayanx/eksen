April 8 (SeeNews) - Bulgarian telecommunications services provider Neterra said on Tuesday that it is now an authorised reseller of Starlink, a satellite internet constellation engineered and operated by Elon Musk's SpaceX aerospace company.

Starlink improves Neterra’s connectivity solutions, offering customers a fast-deployment option available within days, Neterra said in a press release.

"Starlink’s low-Earth orbit ensures fast connections with minimal latency, essential for modern businesses. Combined with Neterra’s global infrastructure, this solution guarantees reliable connectivity even in remote locations," Neterra founder and CEO Neven Dilkov said.

Sofia-headquartered Neterra, founded in 1996, delivers dedicated Internet connectivity and Internet exchange services across over 220 global points of presence. The company provides connectivity, data centre, cybersecurity and IT services to nearly 1,000 global enterprises, as well as nine of the world’s top ten telecom companies.

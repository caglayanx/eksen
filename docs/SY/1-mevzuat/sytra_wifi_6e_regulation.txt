SyTRA introduces new regulation for WiFi 6E technology
The Syrian Telecommunications Regulatory Authority (SyTRA) has introduced a new regulation for WiFi 6E technology, aimed at enhancing wireless connectivity. Key updates include:

Frequency Allocation: WiFi 6E can now operate in the following bands: 2400–2483.5 MHz, 5150–5250 MHz, 5250–5350 MHz, 5470–5725 MHz, 5725–5850 MHz, 5925–6425 MHz, and 57–66 GHz.

Technical Parameters:

Indoor Use: Maximum EIRP of 100–200 mW depending on the band.

Outdoor Use: Maximum EIRP of 1W for the 5725–5850 MHz band.

57–66 GHz: Maximum EIRP of 10W for indoor installations.

Device Compliance: All WiFi 6E devices must comply with SyTRA’s specifications and require type approval before use or import.

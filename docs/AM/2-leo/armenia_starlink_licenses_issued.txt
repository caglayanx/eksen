Armenia's regulator issues licences to Elon Musk's Starlink to operate in the country
YEREVAN, 13 December. /ARKA/. Armenian Minister of High Technology Industry Mkhitar Hayrapetyan said in a Facebook post today that the Public Services Regulatory Commission (PSRC) approved two applications submitted by Starlink AM LLC at meetings on 4 and 13 December.

The first application is for the issuance of a license to operate a public electronic communications network.

The second one is for the issuance of a licence to use radio frequency bands on a secondary basis to establish a public electronic communications network used by global satellite communications systems.

'So, the licenses and permits have been issued and now we are moving to the testing phase, which we hope will be completed and the service will be fully launched in the coming months,’ Hayrapetyan wrote.-0-
